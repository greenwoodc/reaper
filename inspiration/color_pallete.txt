The Next.js App Palette
1. Primary Brand Color:

Next Black: #000000

Use: Global background, text, and branding elements. The definitive Next.js look.

2. Secondary/Accent Color:

Vercel Cyan: #0070F3

Use: Primary buttons (CTAs), links, active states, and focus indicators. Provides high contrast and energy.

3. Neutral Grays (Dark Mode UI):

Background Gray: #111111 (Slightly lighter than black for depth)

Surface Gray: #1A1A1A (For cards, nav bars, and containers)

Border Gray: #333333 (For subtle dividers and borders)

Subtle Text Gray: #888888 (For secondary information)

High-Contrast Text: #EDEDED (Off-white for primary text readability)

4. Status/Feedback Colors:

Success Green: #00D764

Error Red: #FF0033

Warning Yellow: #FFAB00
